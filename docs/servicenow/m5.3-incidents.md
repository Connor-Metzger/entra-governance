# M5.3 — Service desk scenarios in ServiceNow (Keystone Fuel & Market)

*Worked in a ServiceNow Personal Developer Instance on 2026-11-16. Built in a lab environment. Exports: `evidence/M5/servicenow/`.*

Each record is either **Real** (a lab event that actually happened, worked after the fact) or **Tabletop** (a realistic scenario worked on paper and in the ticket, nothing changed in Entra). Narrative times are in the work notes; ServiceNow's own timestamps are the day the ticket was keyed.

## Priority rule (ServiceNow impact × urgency)

| | Urgency 1 High | Urgency 2 Medium | Urgency 3 Low |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Impact 1 High** (PCI-scope access exposed, or a security control down) | P1 Critical | P2 High | P3 Moderate |
| **Impact 2 Medium** (a store or a team can't work) | P2 High | P3 Moderate | P4 Low |
| **Impact 3 Low** (one person, workaround exists) | P3 Moderate | P4 Low | P5 Planning |

Urgency 1 = harm is happening now or a hard deadline is under 4 hours away. Keystone stores run 24/7, so "it's the middle of the night" never lowers urgency.

## Assignment groups
- **IAM Operations**: desk and on-call analyst (L1/L2). Works the ticket.
- **IAM Engineering**: workflow, package and review configuration (L3).
- **Security Operations**: anything that may be an attack or misuse. Owns the investigation; IAM acts on its instruction.
- **HR Service Center**: owns people data (hires, terminations, managers).

For staff who call the desk instead of using My Access. **ServiceNow holds the request and approvals; IAM fulfils in Entra** and puts the RITM number in the assignment justification so the two records link.

| Variable | Type | Mandatory | Why |

| Business reason | Multi line text | Yes | What, which store, until when (policy §8) |

| I do **not** hold POS Refunds | Checkbox | Yes | SoD with ADD-01 (policy §5) |
